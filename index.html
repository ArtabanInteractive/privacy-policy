Privacy Policy

Artaban Interactive operates this game. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use the game.

Information We Collect

The game does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

The game uses Google's advertising services, including the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. AdMob may automatically collect certain information from your device and your interaction with the game for purposes including displaying and measuring advertisements, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

This may include information such as:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • IP address and general network information
  • Information about interactions with the game and advertisements
  • Diagnostic and performance information
  • Information related to advertising and app usage
  • Purchase-related information where applicable to the services used by the game

The information collected by third-party advertising services is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

How We Use Information

Information collected through the game and its third-party services may be used to:

  • Provide and display advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Understand and improve game performance
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Maintain and improve the game and related services

Third-Party Services

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), a third-party advertising service provided by Google. AdMob may collect and process information as described in Google's documentation and privacy policies.

Google's privacy policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

Information transmitted by the advertising services used by the game is protected using appropriate security measures. Google Mobile Ads data is encrypted in transit using TLS.

Data Retention and Deletion

Artaban Interactive does not maintain user accounts and does not maintain a personal user database for the game.

Information collected by third-party services such as AdMob may be retained according to those services' applicable policies and retention practices.

Because the game does not provide user accounts, there is no account deletion process required for the game.

Children's Privacy

The game is intended for users aged 18 and over. The game does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Your Choices

Users may have options provided by their device, Google, or advertising services to manage advertising preferences and identifiers. These options may include resetting or limiting the use of advertising identifiers through Android settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
